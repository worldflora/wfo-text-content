## Brazilian Flora

This Brazilian flora dataset is an extracted subset of the Brazilian Flora 2020 project (Projeto Flora do Brasil 2020), containing only a portion rather than the full checklist. It is based on a collaborative checklist of Brazil's plants, algae, and fungi produced by around 700 taxonomists under the REFLORA Program at the Instituto de Pesquisas Jardim Botânico do Rio de Janeiro.

The source dataset covers 46,485 species (as of early 2018) with nomenclatural information, distribution within Brazil, endemism, biome coverage, life forms, substrate, and vegetation types. It is published as a GBIF checklist under a CC BY 4.0 license. The source dataset is available at https://doi.org/10.15468/1mtkaw and the project homepage is http://floradobrasil.jbrj.gov.br/. The preferred citation is: Brazil Flora G (2014). Brazilian Flora 2020 project. v393.147. Instituto de Pesquisas Jardim Botânico do Rio de Janeiro. doi:10.15468/1mtkaw.
