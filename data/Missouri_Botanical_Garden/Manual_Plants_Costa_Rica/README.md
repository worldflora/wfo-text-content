## Manual de Plantas de Costa Rica

Hammel, B. E., Grayum, M. H., Herrera, C., & Zamora, N. (Eds.). (2003–2020). Manual de Plantas de Costa Rica (Monographs in Systematic Botany from the Missouri Botanical Garden). Missouri Botanical Garden Press.

This data is derived from the Manual de Plantas de Costa Rica, a collaboration between Missouri Botanical Garden, the Instituto Nacional de Biodiversidad, and the Museo Nacional de Costa Rica. The printed series was published in eight volumes between 2003 and 2020, edited by B. E. Hammel, M. H. Grayum, C. Herrera, and N. Zamora, and was issued as part of the Monographs in Systematic Botany from the Missouri Botanical Garden Series. A companion online database is hosted by MBG.

Content is organized by family, genus, and species, with color photographs, formal descriptions, informal notes, and identification keys at the genus and species levels.
