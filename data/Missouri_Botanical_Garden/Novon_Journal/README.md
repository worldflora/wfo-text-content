## Novon Journal

Novon is a quarterly peer-reviewed botanical journal from Missouri Botanical Garden Press, published since 1991, with articles in English or Spanish. It establishes new nomenclature and typifies existing names for vascular plants and bryophytes as opposed to broader taxonomic revisions. It's a companion to the Annals of the Missouri Botanical Garden and shows up regularly in POWO and IPNI as a place-of-publication reference for new plant names.
