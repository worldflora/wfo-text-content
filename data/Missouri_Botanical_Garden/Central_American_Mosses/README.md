## Central American Mosses Flora

Allen, B. (1994–2018). Moss flora of Central America (Parts 1–4, Monographs in Systematic Botany from the Missouri Botanical Garden). Missouri Botanical Garden Press.

This data was provided by Missouri Botanical Garden, including over 700 moss species from Belize, Guatemala, El Salvador, Honduras, Nicaragua, Costa Rica, and Panama.

Genera and species treatment include the place of original publication, general synonymy, a brief description, and type information where applicable. Family level treatment mirrors the phylogenetic sequence used in Bartram's *Mosses of Guatemala* (1949).
