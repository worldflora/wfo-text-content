## Flora Mesoamericana

This data was provided by Missouri Botanical Garden and it describes all vascular flora found south of Mexico. The result of a collaborative effort between Missouri Botanical Garden and the Instituto de Biología of the National Autonomous University of Mexico, the Flora Mesoamericana was originally published in printed volumes from 1994 to 1995.

The species treatment includes information on place of publication, typification, synonymy, and representative specimen citations for accepted names.
