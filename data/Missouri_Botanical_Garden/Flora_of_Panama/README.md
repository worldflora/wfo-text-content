## Flora of Panama

D'Arcy, W. G. (1987). Flora of Panama (Monographs in Systematic Botany from the Missouri Botanical Garden Vol. 17, Parts 1–3). Missouri Botanical Garden.

Based on the Flora of Panama, the first major overseas floristic project of the Missouri Botanical Garden. Formally begun by Robert Woodson and colleagues in 1943, the project concluded with the publication of the Flora of Panama Checklist and Index by William G. D'Arcy in 1987, issued in three parts as volume 17 of the Monographs in Systematic Botany from the Missouri Botanical Garden series. The Checklist covers all vascular plant species known to occur in Panama, comprising 7,345 species across 195 families as of 1987. Entries include habit and distribution information for each species.
