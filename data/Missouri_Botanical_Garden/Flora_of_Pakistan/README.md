## Flora of Pakistan

This dataset of over 4,000 species is based on the Flora of Pakistan Project, a comprehensive inventory of vascular plants of Pakistan published by University of Karachi since 1968 and in collaboration with Missouri Botanical Garden since 2000. Original Joint Editors were E. Nasir and S. I. Ali, with M. Qaiser succeeding Nasir after his death. Individual publications have been authored by the editors, their colleagues and students, and by specialist contributors worldwide. The flora was published in a series of just over 200 printed fascicles by 2005 and later as an online database.

Treatments occur on the family, genera, and species levels, including descriptions, keys, synonymies, distribution, place of publication, typification, use, phenology, specimen citations, and illustrations where applicable. The flora contains no endemic families, but three endemic genera: Douepia, Stewartiella, and Decalepidanthus.
