## Flora of West Tropical Africa

This dataset was provided by the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew in 2023 and is derived from a series of books published since the 1930s. The flora includes species from Mauritania, Senegal, Gambia, Mali, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana (Gold Coast), Togo, Benin (Dahomey), Niger, Nigeria, Cameroon, and Equatorial Guinea (Bioko).
