This dataset was provided by Conservatory and Botanical Garden of the City of Geneva (CJBG) in November 2025 and is derived from the Flora Helvetica print series, describing over 3,000 botanical species belonging to Switzerland. The latest 6th edition text has been augmented with additions from the Info Flora Checklist of the vascular flora of Switzerland 2017, and was published in 2024 through Haupt Verlag (Bern, Switzerland).

Citations:

Lauber, K., Wagner, G., & Gygax, A. (2024). Flora Helvetica: Illustrated Flora of Switzerland (6th ed.). Haupt Verlag.

Juillerat, P., Bäumler, B., Bornand, C., Gygax, A., Jutzi, M., Möhl, A., Nyffeler, R., Sager, L., Santiago, H., & Eggenberg, S. (2017). Checklist 2017 der Gefässpflanzenflora der Schweiz [Checklist 2017 of the vascular flora of Switzerland] (Info Flora, Ed.). Info Flora.
