## Flora of West Tropical Africa

This dataset was provided in 2023 by the Royal Botanic Gardens, Kew, whose Herbarium prepared the Flora of West Tropical Africa: a multi-volume flora first published in the late 1920s and revised through the 1970s. The flora includes species from Mauritania, Senegal, Gambia, Mali, Guinea-Bissau, Guinea, Sierra Leone, Liberia, Ivory Coast, Ghana (Gold Coast), Togo, Benin (Dahomey), Niger, Nigeria, Cameroon, and Equatorial Guinea (Bioko).

Citations:
Hutchinson, J., & Dalziel, J. M. (1927–1972). Flora of West Tropical Africa. Crown Agents for Overseas Governments and Administrations.
